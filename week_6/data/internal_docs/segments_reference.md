# Customer segments reference

Maintained by Marketing Analytics. Used for campaign targeting and email lists.

- **Weekend Hikers**: customers with 1-3 orders in the past year, average order value under $200, primarily browsing day-hiking gear (backpacks, footwear, light layers).
- **Trail Runners**: customers who have purchased running-specific or ultralight gear (rain shells, hydration vests) and engage with trail-running content.
- **Gear Collectors**: customers with 4+ orders in the past year across multiple categories, high average order value, early adopters of new product launches.
